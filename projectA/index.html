[ Neon Planarian ]

Scientific Name:

Neonarian

Discovered by / at:

Luna,2026

Family:

The creature is made of light and usually appeared in groups. They die and reborn quickly and creat colorful lights in the sky.

Habitat:

The creature live in the deep universe. They enjoy large space to interact with each other. But they hate air and water and humans.

Appearance:

Overall, the creature has a random oval appearance, and have different colors. They breath as they move. The color is deeper in the centre of the creature. Their small eyes open and close as human scientists try to interact with them.

Key Characteristics:

  • Float in the dark.
  • Get energy from stars.
  • Die fast and reborn.
  • leave rings when they die.
